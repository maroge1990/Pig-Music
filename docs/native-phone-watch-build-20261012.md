# iPhone/iPad parity candidate and production Watch app

12 October 2026, Australia/Melbourne. Build 0.2.0 (2). This milestone extends the October 11 testing candidate. The owner requested completion of everyday iPhone features and a working Watch app, including independent downloads, gapless playback and crossfade. Publishing to main is authorized; live Unraid deployment is separate. Software delivery and physical acceptance are recorded separately below.

## Bounds and platform policy

- Phone: iOS/iPadOS 18+, originals default; download queue at most 10,000 songs; OS scheduling window 128 prepared transfers with two simultaneous HTTP connections. Complete queued AAC preparation is not guaranteed while the phone app is suspended. Force-quitting an app is distinct from ordinary backgrounding; Apple may defer/cancel background work until reopening.
- Seamless transient cache: two leased files, 128 MiB per remote file, 256 MiB total. Slow transfers or unsupported originals can need streaming fallback or a downloaded AAC copy. Audible codec/route behavior remains a device gate.
- Watch: watchOS 11+, 1 GiB default music budget, configurable 1/2/4/8 GiB, at most 1,000 selected songs, 256 MiB per AAC file, 200 mirrored collections and 10,000 playlist occurrences in aggregate. Playlists mirror only selected songs; repeated occurrences remain repeated. Manifest limit 2 MiB; inbox 512 MiB. Oversized metadata reports an error. The paired phone retains a separate AAC staging archive (2 GiB initially, expanding to the acknowledged Watch budget up to 8 GiB), in addition to ordinary phone downloads. Removing the Watch selection removes its staged phone copy. The phone bridge supports the paired iPhone; iPad does not pair with Apple Watch.
- Watch downloads require the paired phone for server access/preparation; downloaded playback does not require the phone or server. Both devices can initiate download/removal management. Remote control requires reachability. System transfer timing is not guaranteed by application code.
- Revocation or membership removal reconciles after a confirmed connection. Offline playback persists during an outage. Confirmed account changes stop playback and clear local access. Reinstalling a client can require rebuilding its selected Watch library; a received Watch revision prevents a fresh phone counter from leaving the bridge permanently stale.
- Offline edit queue: 1,000 pending operations per person. Server keeps up to 100,000 mutation receipts per person and does not silently expire deduplication evidence. At that limit it requires administrator review. A future retention protocol must account for every device's replay window.
- Qualified listening history records one play after 30 seconds of actual playing time per occurrence. Seeking does not fabricate listening time. Full overlap-aware duration analytics, cross-device queue handoff, standalone Watch server login, CarPlay, advanced import matching and Mac remain future improvements rather than acceptance claims for this build.

The final standalone production Watch Release simulator build also passes. Version 0.2.0 (2) installed, launched and rendered its sign-in screen on a disposable iPhone 16 Pro Max / iOS 27 simulator. The QA simulator was removed afterward. Interactive journeys could not run while the Mac was locked. Software PCM capture and compilation do not establish physical headphone/background behavior.

## Installation and remaining acceptance

The test devices are iPhone 16 Pro Max (seen by Xcode on iOS 27.0.1) and Watch Ultra 3 (owner reports watchOS 27). The Watch is not yet exposed to Xcode; the owner will enable Developer Mode after software build completion. The installed Apple Development certificate alone is insufficient: signing currently fails with **No Accounts** and missing provisioning profiles for both app bundles. Add/sign into the free Apple Account in Xcode Settings → Accounts, then select its Personal Team with automatic signing. Keep signing/account changes local.

Phone target **PigMusic** embeds **PigMusicWatch**. If local bundle identifiers change, update the Watch identifier and its `WKCompanionAppBundleIdentifier` together. Free Personal Team profiles expire and require rebuilding; see [Apple account guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/basics/about-your-developer-account).

Required device journeys before marking parity accepted:

1. Install/update and reopen on phone and tablet; verify owner/member separation, provider fallback, accessible navigation/large text, 50k search/paging, playlists/import review, settings, conflicts and uncertain-response replay.
2. Capture continuous-album and crossfade boundaries across actual AAC/MP3/FLAC/mixed-rate sources. Verify repeat, pause/seek, lock-screen controls, interruptions, Bluetooth/AirPlay, background timing and storage pressure. Test explicitly selected streaming fallback.
3. Schedule original/AAC transfers, background/lock/reopen, network and cellular policy changes, interrupted resume, process relaunch, low storage, quality replacement, source replacement and confirmed revocation. Do not substitute a foreground simulator transfer for OS scheduling proof.
4. Install on the paired Ultra 3, push from phone and request from Watch, lower the wrist/background during delivery, retry after disconnection/restart, remove and redownload, then turn the phone off and verify headphone playback, gapless/crossfade, repeat, seeks and interruptions. Reconnect and verify history and access reconciliation, user switch/re-pair and delayed-transfer rejection.
5. Test matching server state and provider acquisition on isolated Unraid/library copies, with backup and rollback. Keep administrative/provider credentials out of Watch transfers and untrusted media requests.

GitHub Actions remains blocked at the account level by billing/spending limits as recorded in the previous handoff. Local checks do not produce a tested Linux image or authorize live deployment. Physical installation, device acceptance and container/runtime acceptance remain open; this document does not declare full parity accepted.
