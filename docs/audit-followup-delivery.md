# Remaining audit implementation — 7 October 2026

This package follows the remaining audit priorities after the deployed A01–A03/B01–B02/T01 work. It is based on the live transport branch, without the unfinished account cutover.

- **P1-3:** card names/captions wrap; section headings and controls accommodate enlarged text. Mobile navigation switches naturally from four columns to two as rem-based text grows. ResizeObserver reserves the actual navigation/player height so fixed controls cannot cover the end of the collection or each other. Decorative placeholder initials are hidden from accessible names. Physical VoiceOver and iPhone acceptance remain unverified.
- **P1-5:** the protected player uses the approved media.solidPanel, media text/accent/onAccent and generated dark raised roles. Existing shadow/scrim opacities are generated from approved media black, retaining their appearance without independent hex values.
- **P1-7:** collection grids show 100 cards per page, with keyboard-operable previous/next controls and a live range announcement. The 50,000-track fixture has 5,000 albums but only 100 album cards in the DOM. Paging replaces content-visibility, preserving complete accessible names for every rendered card; no cards disappear merely because focus/scroll moves.
- **P2-3:** newly cached images receive deterministic, hash-keyed dominant-colour/dimension sidecars. Existing images backfill through bounded local artwork workers or the metadata endpoint. Projection refresh reads small cached sidecars and never decodes images. The immutable metadata endpoint supports conditional reads. These are artwork data, not new product colours; the branded placeholder and approved player palette are retained. Safe pruning includes orphan metadata after the established grace period.
- **P2-7:** collection card rendering and pagination/styles move into components; immutable artwork and metadata routes move into a dedicated router. Wider frontend/settings/provider/store extraction remains incremental work.
- **P3:** redundant saved ticks are removed from personal artist cards. Starlette's documented httpx2 test client is pinned with its new dependencies; production provider clients remain on the existing httpx dependency. The deprecation is resolved rather than suppressed.

Validation: 147 backend tests passed without the former warning. After route extraction, 13 focused artwork/brand tests passed. Three frontend tests, strict TypeScript/production build, formatting and generated-token drift checks passed. A disposable 50,000-track browser fixture verified 100 rendered cards, complete labels at 200% text, keyboard page advancement and non-overlapping measured player/navigation at 375×812. Physical devices, real VoiceOver, home-screen installation, cellular/Tailscale and the Sideloadly Watch spike remain outstanding. The owner reports the spike is not installed.

No audio files, tags, household accounts or live settings are changed by implementation. Release candidate publication and exact-image Unraid acceptance are tracked separately; the previously deployed image remains brand-https-20261007-v1 until that succeeds.
